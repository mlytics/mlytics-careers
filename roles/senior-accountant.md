# Senior Accountant

## Job responsibilities

- Handle daily payment operations, banking activities, and cash management processes.
- Perform regular bank reconciliations and account balance reviews.
- Assist in month-end and quarter-end closing, including journal entries and expense recognition.
- Manage invoice processing and employee expense reimbursements.
- Support the preparation of financial reports and accounting analysis.
- Assist in annual budget planning and variance analysis.
- Coordinate with external parties including banks, auditors, and tax advisors.
- Perform other ad hoc accounting or finance-related duties assigned by supervisors.

## Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
- At least 3 years of relevant experience in audit or tax teams at a Big Four accounting firm, and at least 1 year of industry experience.
- Proficiency in Excel and financial systems (e.g., QuickBooks, SAP, Oracle).
- Detail-oriented, responsible, with strong communication and teamwork skills.
- Ability to read/write English financial documents is a plus.

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience in startups or multinational environments.
- Familiarity with Taiwan tax regulations and compliance processes.
